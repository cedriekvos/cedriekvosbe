The rule

The constraint the coder must honour, as concretely as possible: a snippet, a class name, a path, a forbidden call. This is the part feature-development comes back for — put it first and keep it short.

php
// a snippet showing exactly what to do, or not do

Why

Three to six sentences: the constraint that forced a choice, and why this option satisfies it. Cite evidence as vendor/some/lib/File.php:42 — do not paste source. Do not narrate how the decision was reached.

Evidence longer than the decision goes in here

Proof, vendor behaviour, measurements. Delete if unused.
