Left off the diagram because they import nothing under App\ and nothing imports them: App\View, App\Models, App\Providers. Laravel resolves them directly.

2 of the 13 arrows do not point straight down. Not necessarily wrong — but these are the couplings that make two modules hard to move independently.
  • App\About → App\Blog (sideways, between two modules in the same band)
    AboutFactory imports PostMarkdownToHtmlConverter
  • App\Security → App\Mail (upward, out of its band)
    VulnerabilityNotifier imports ComposerVulnerabilityAlert
App\Mail and App\Security import each other. A module-level cycle: neither can be understood, moved or tested without the other.
