# Leesmij

## Keep each leesmij to its essence
A leesmij is a short Dutch note on the why: a one- or two-sentence intro, then one bullet per decision with its reason (`- **Keuze** — waarom.`). No history or process narration (task numbers, "tot nu toe", earlier rounds), no class names or file paths, no restated Gherkin. When behavior changes, rewrite the note to the current state instead of appending a section. Wrap at 120 characters.
